DEPARTMENT CHRONICLES

Behind the Lens: Moments & Milestones

A quiet look back at the trips, trials, and shared breakthroughs that shaped our year.

04 CHAPTERS
Field Trip • 2024 – 2025

A Trip to the Defence Expo: The 60-Meal Feat

Thanks to the state government, our department got the chance to head out to Nandambakkam. The exhibitions were fascinating, but the true behind-the-scenes triumph was logistical: diplomatic persistence, quick coordination, and somehow talking with event coordinators until all 60 of us walked away with free lunch boxes.

Exhibit & Research • 2024 – 2025

Watching Our Seniors Take Flight

Walking across the expo floor and seeing our own college banner was a distinct highlight. Our aerospace and research seniors were stationed right in the thick of it, presenting mini-satellite modules and autonomous drone systems. It grounded the theory we study into something tangible and flying.

Community Outreach • January 2026

Math Is Fun... Apparently

Supported by local initiatives, we took outreach away from quiet blackboards and straight into hands-on games. We turned combinatorics and logic into energetic party games with unexpected rules. It was loud, slightly unhinged, thoroughly messy, and had students laughing while solving math without realizing it.

Guest Lecture • February 2026

National Math Day: Sampling & Reality

To mark National Math Day, we hosted an IIT professor who broke down random selection and sampling theory. Instead of abstract proofs, we explored real-world quality control, material defect testing, and data verification—demonstrating how basic probability safeguards industrial manufacturing.
