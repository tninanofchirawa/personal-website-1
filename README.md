## 📖 The Story Behind This Site

It all started with a simple Google Form sent to friends and seniors at **Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science (HITS)** about college stress and sleep habits.

77 students took the time to answer. But when it came to compiling the numbers and building custom charts in Google Sheets, it took five solid, exhausting hours of wrestling with cells and formulas.

Looking back at that screen late at night, a clear realization struck:

> _"I can't expect students to share their honest data with me if they never get to see what happens to it. True learning needs transparency."_

Thus, this website was _born_. ☺️

Over time, this space also became a place to document the journey at HITS—sharing the small events, student initiatives, and club activities we organized and participated in along the way.
