    📖 The Story Behind This Site
  </h2>

It all started with a simple Google Form sent to friends and seniors at
<strong style="color: #e6c66a;">
Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science (HITS)
</strong>
about college stress and sleep habits.

77 students took the time to answer. But when it came to compiling the
numbers and building custom charts in Google Sheets, it took five solid,
exhausting hours of wrestling with cells and formulas.

Looking back at that screen late at night, a clear realization struck:

  <blockquote style="border-left: 4px solid #d4af37;
                     background: rgba(212,175,55,0.07);
                     padding: 14px 20px;
                     margin: 22px 0;
                     border-radius: 0 8px 8px 0;
                     font-family: Georgia, serif;
                     font-style: italic;
                     color: #e9d9a5;">

    "I can't expect students to share their honest data with me
    if they never get to see what happens to it.
    True learning needs transparency."

  </blockquote>

Thus, this website was <em style="color: #e6c66a;">born</em>. ☺️

  <br />

Over time, this space also became a place to document the journey at HITS—
sharing the small events, student initiatives, and club activities we
organized and participated in along the way.
