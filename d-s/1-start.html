A Little Behind the Scenes 🔍

Here's what I'm hoping to explore:

  • 🔹 How study hours and preparation habits relate to exam performance.
  • 🔹 Whether sleep, consistency, and exam stress make a difference.
  • 🔹 Using mathematical models and data visualisations to make sense of the responses.

No two students prepare quite the same way. I'm curious to see what we can learn from everyone's experiences — without assuming that there's one perfect way to study! 😊
