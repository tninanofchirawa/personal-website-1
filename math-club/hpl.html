← Back to Math Club

The Mathematics Department conducted the inaugural Hindustan Premier League (HPL) event for first-year B.Tech students. What started as an ambitious experiment to blend cricket fever with mathematical formulas turned into an unforgettable whirlwind of problem-solving, teamwork, and sheer adrenaline.

Phase 01

A Peek Behind the Scenes

People usually only see the final event on match day… but what happens behind the curtains is a whole different story 😭. It takes an incredible amount of planning, and then even more planning 😵‍💫📋. You end up calculating every possible hurdle before it even happens 😑💭.

The organizing team worked across literally every front:

  • 🎥 Video Making: Scripting, recording, and editing teasers to build campus buzz.
  • 📢 Promotions & Outreach: Classroom visits, posters, and spreading the word.
  • 📝 Question Bank Curation: Crafting balance-tested cricket math puzzles and problem sets.
  • 👥 Crowd Coordination: Managing massive batches of enthusiastic participants.
  • 📄 Official Approvals: Handling formal letters, department requisitions, and faculty sign-offs.
  • 🏫 Venue Logistics: Securing halls, sound systems, and scheduling rooms.
  • 💡 Format Iteration: Polishing rules so cricket excitement and mathematical rigor stayed balanced… and honestly, so much more 🥳🔥

Huge thanks to our professors for helping us with permissions, approvals, brainstorming, and constant support throughout 🙌✨. It was stressful, chaotic, and tiring… but also super fun and completely worth every minute in the end 💯✨!

Phase 02

Turning K.S. Moorthy Hall into a Stadium

The juniors know it was an absolute blast 🎉🔥. At one point, our professors were genuinely worried that we were turning K.S. Moorthy Hall into a full-on marketplace 😂!

The Student Affairs Dean and the Dean of Academics even dropped by to check everything out 👀🏫. But the energy, excitement, and genuine sportsmanship were so infectious that they gave us the green signal on the spot and told us not to worry about the noise ✨.

Man… the whole thing was a complete roller coaster 🎢… but somehow, through all the madness, everything came together 🥳. Huge thanks to all our professors for trusting us, backing our ideas, and helping make this inaugural edition a reality 🙏.
