← Back to Math Club

The Mathematics Department launched the inaugural Hindustan Premier League (HPL) for first-year B.Tech students. We had just started out, diving headfirst into an uncharted concept to fuse cricket passion with math puzzles. It turned into a chaotic, unforgettable whirlwind—and the exact moment we realized we had struck gold and had to take it even further next year.

Phase 01

Starting from Scratch: Behind the Curtains

People usually only see the final buzz on match day… but what happens behind the curtains when you’re building something for the very first time is a whole different story 😭. We were starting from absolute zero, which meant planning, second-guessing, and then planning even more 😵‍💫📋. You end up calculating every single hurdle before it even hits the radar 😑💭.

Since we had no prior script to follow, the team worked across literally every front:

  • 🎥 Video Making: Scripting, recording, and editing teasers on the fly to build campus buzz.
  • 📢 Promotions & Outreach: Pitching directly during classroom visits, putting up posters, and rallying the crowd.
  • 📝 Question Bank Curation: Designing brand-new cricket math puzzles and balance-testing each problem set from scratch.
  • 👥 Crowd Coordination: Figuring out how to herd and manage massive batches of energetic first-years.
  • 📄 Official Approvals: Drafting department letters, requisitions, and getting faculty sign-offs.
  • 🏫 Venue Logistics: Reserving halls, sorting AV systems, and coordinating mics.
  • 💡 Format Iteration: Hammering out the rules so cricket strategy and mathematical rigor stayed balanced… and so much more 🥳🔥

Huge shoutout to our professors for backing us with permissions, brainstorming, and constant encouragement right from day one 🙌✨. It was stressful, chaotic, and exhausting… but seeing it take shape was completely worth every minute 💯✨!

Phase 02

Turning K.S. Moorthy Hall into a Stadium

The crowd brought sheer energy 🎉🔥. At one point, our professors were genuinely concerned we were turning K.S. Moorthy Hall into a noisy marketplace 😂!

The Student Affairs Dean and the Dean of Academics even walked in to inspect the commotion 👀🏫. But once they felt the electric atmosphere, the high-stakes problem-solving, and the genuine sportsmanship in the room, they gave us the green light on the spot and told us not to worry about the volume ✨.

The whole day was a complete roller coaster 🎢. We were figuring out parts of it live, patching things together on our feet, and watching the room light up with every solved question. That was the moment it clicked: we had just started something special, and we walked out of that hall knowing 100% that the next year would be bigger, sharper, and on a completely different level 🚀.
