The Mathematics Department conducted the HPL event for the first year B.Tech students.

A little peek behind the scenes 👀✨

People usually only see the final event…
but what happens behind it is a whole different story 😭
It takes a LOT of planning…
and then even more planning 😵‍💫📋
You end up thinking about every possible problem before it even happens 😑💭

The teams work on literally everything:

  • 🎥 Video making
  • 📢 Advertisements & promotions
  • 📝 Creating the question bank
  • 👥 Crowd handling & coordination
  • 📨 Going around inviting participants
  • 📄 Collecting letters and approvals
  • 🏫 Asking permissions for rooms
  • 💡 Building and improving the main idea
    …and honestly, so much more 🥳🔥

Huge thanks to the professors for helping us with permissions, approvals, brainstorming, and constant support throughout 🙌✨

It’s stressful, chaotic, tiring…
but also super fun and completely worth it in the end 💯✨

The juniors know it was an absolute blast 🎉🔥
At one point, our professors were genuinely worried that we were turning the K.S. Moorthy Hall into a full-on marketplace 😂
The Student Affairs Dean and the Dean of Academics even came to check everything out 👀🏫 But the energy, excitement, and fun were so good that they gave the green signal and told us not to worry about the noise✨
Man… the whole thing was a complete roller coaster 🎢… but somehow everything came together in the end 🥳
Huge thanks to all the professors for trusting us, supporting us, and helping make this happen 🙏
