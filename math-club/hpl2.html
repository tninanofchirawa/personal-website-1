← Back to Math Club

HPL 2.0 was never just a cricket-themed math event to us. It was a wild ride of crazy ideas, late-night teamwork, curveballs we never saw coming, and figuring out how to keep pushing forward when nothing went according to script.

Phase 01

The Masterplan: Building the Dream App

Before HPL 2.0, we had our first run with HPL 1.0. That first edition gave us the blueprint—it showed us what clicked and where we could step things up. For the sequel, we wanted to take it to another level: real mathematical thinking, high-stakes budgeting, squad strategies, and team dynamics right in the mix 🏏

As juniors, we had what felt like a bulletproof ambition: build a custom live-auction app from scratch to surprise our seniors and make the event ridiculously interactive. We roped in some faculty and CSE seniors to help code it out, ironed out almost all the bugs, and got it practically ready to deploy. From our perspective, the plan was flawless: it would be blazing fast, dead simple to manage, eliminate manual arithmetic calculations, and keep everyone hooked. We couldn't wait to pitch the finished masterpiece.

Phase 02

The Reality Check & The Hard Pivot

Then came pitch day. We presented the app, fully expecting applause—and the seniors shot it down in record time. Looking back, we had worked with tunnel vision, blindly obsessed with what we wanted to build rather than what the room actually needed. Senior laid down the law without hesitation: "We are not bringing them all into the hall just to play a video game." Then came the cold engineering reality: network infrastructure. How would 100+ phones connect simultaneously inside the hall without crashing everything? No basic setup could survive dozens of students spamming auction clicks in real time. We had spent weeks building a dream solution to a problem nobody asked for, and just like that, our "bulletproof" tech stack evaporated.

Gameplan changed instantly. We stepped out of our developer bubble and traded the fancy app for raw adrenaline: good old Google Sheets, projector slides, fast mental math, and organizers shouting bid values across K. S. Moorthy Hall. It wasn't the slick digital pipeline we had poured hours into coding, but it was exactly what the hall needed to run. The show couldn't stop—and honestly, that forced pivot made the event legendary 💡

Phase 03

Match Day & Calculated Chaos

Forty teams packed the hall for the preliminary round, tackling 30 grueling questions combining cricket instincts with math grit—net run rates, required strike rates, compound percentages, and probability.

Once the dust settled, the top ten squads earned their seats at the auction table. With budgets on the line, role quotas to meet, and paddles flying, the room shifted from quiet problem-solving straight into pure, calculated chaos!

Phase 03

What Really Mattered

Looking back at it all, HPL 2.0 was never just about point tables, player cards, or bidding paddles. It was really about the team behind the scenes—the frantic planning chats, the blunt reality checks, fixing mistakes on the fly, and every single friend who stepped in to make it happen.

Not a single stage went strictly according to script. But we learned how to listen to hard advice, adapt when our grand plans hit the ground, and back each other up under pressure. Pulling off an ambitious idea doesn't mean having a flawless first draft—it means having the grit to pivot and see it through together 🙌

Above All, Thank You, Jesus.

Through every blueprint, every panic moment, and every lesson learned, my heart is deeply grateful to God. Huge thanks to our professors for trusting and guiding us, to our friends who stood shoulder-to-shoulder, and to the grace that kept us steady through every turn.

By God's grace. For the glory of Jesus.
