← Back to Math Club

HPL 2.0 was never just a cricket-themed math event to us. It was a wild ride of crazy ideas, late-night teamwork, curveballs we never saw coming, and figuring out how to keep pushing forward when nothing went according to script.

Phase 01

The Spark & The Big Ambition

Before HPL 2.0, we had our first run with HPL 1.0. That first edition gave us the blueprint—it showed us what clicked, what fell flat, and where we could step things up. For the sequel, we didn't want ordinary cricket trivia. We wanted to throw real mathematical thinking, high-stakes budgeting, squad strategies, and team dynamics right into the mix. And just like that, HPL 2.0 was born 🏏

The concept sounded straightforward enough: bring the heart-racing excitement of an IPL mega-auction straight into our Mathematics Department. Pulling that off in real life? A whole different ball game. We had question papers to draft, slide decks to polish, rules to iron out, endless forms to sort, permissions to chase down, and a million moving parts to coordinate. We even set out to build custom live-scoring software for the auction bids. The ambition was sky-high—and the deadline was racing toward us.

Phase 02

The Clutch Pivot & Match Day

Right when the clock was running down, reality hit: our custom software simply wasn't stable enough to endure the chaos of a live crowd. So, we pivoted on the fly. Good old spreadsheets, slides, fast mental arithmetic, and nonstop shouting across the room became our lifeline. It definitely wasn't the slick digital pipeline we’d drawn up, but the show simply couldn't stop. That moment gave us a lesson every student organizer learns sooner or later: a solid plan is great, but knowing how to roll with the punches when Plan A cracks is what really saves the day 💡

On the day of the event, forty teams packed K. S. Moorthy Hall for the preliminary round. Thirty questions tested both deep cricket instincts and math grit—covering net run rates, required strike rates, compound percentages, and probability. Once the dust settled, only the top ten teams earned their seats at the auction table, and the vibe instantly shifted from solving equations to ruthless, fast-paced decision making.

Suddenly, every squad had a purse to manage, role quotas to satisfy, and nerve-wracking bidding wars to navigate. Spend big on one marquee name, and the rest of your lineup paid the price. It was strategy meeting math head-on, and the auction room delivered pure, calculated chaos!

Phase 03

What Really Mattered

Looking back at it all, HPL 2.0 was never just about point tables, player cards, or bidding paddles. It was really about the team behind the scenes—the frantic planning chats, the honest debates, fixing mistakes on the fly, and every single friend who volunteered a hand to bring this dream off paper.

Not a single stage went completely seamless. But we learned how to listen, how to stay calm when things felt unglued, and how to back each other up. Honestly, that was the biggest takeaway: pulling off an ambitious idea doesn't mean having a flawless run—it means having the grit to see it through together 🙌
