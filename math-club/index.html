The Mathematics Club at HITS had quite the eventful 2025–26 academic year 🚀. Between HPL, SDG, mathematics competitions, field trips, training camps, and outreach activities, there was certainly no shortage of things to do — or excuses to say, "We'll manage it somehow." 😌

✨ This page brings together some of the highlights from the year. Not every event has made it here (even this website has its limits, unlike some of our seniors' confidence 😏), but there's enough to give our seniors a chance to relive the memories and our juniors a glimpse of what they've signed up for. Consider it a recap, a record, and perhaps a gentle warning. 😉

I've created detailed pages for events where I had the opportunity to contribute, whether in a leading or supporting role, including HPL 🏏, SDG 🌍, and the Mathematics Training Camp 📖. Other activities, such as National Mathematics Day celebrations, outings, and various competitions, are featured here as well. Together, they capture a little of the hard work, teamwork, last-minute arrangements, and occasional chaos that make the Mathematics Club what it is. To our seniors: thank you for setting the standards (and the expectations). To our juniors: all the best — you'll understand soon enough. 😌
