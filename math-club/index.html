The Mathematics Club at HITS had a busy and exciting 2025–26 academic year 🚀. From HPL and SDG to mathematics competitions, field trips, training camps, outreach activities, and much more, the year was packed with opportunities to learn, collaborate, and grow.

✨This page is a collection of some of the highlights from that journey. While it doesn't capture every event, it provides a glimpse into the many activities, achievements, and experiences that made this year memorable for our students.

I've created detailed pages for events where I had the opportunity to contribute in either a leading or supporting role, including HPL 🏏, SDG 🌍, and the Mathematics Training Camp 📖. Other activities such as National Mathematics Day celebrations, outings, and various competitions are also featured here. Together, they showcase just a small part of the enthusiasm, hard work, and community spirit that define the Mathematics Club.
