The School of Basic and Applied Sciences hosts the National Science Fair every year, and our team secured 2nd place in the 2025 edition 🏆🔥. Proof that mathematicians occasionally step out of their notebooks and into the real world. 😌📐

We presented our Tamil Nadu State Championship Math Project 2025 📚✨ with plenty of confidence — only to be outperformed by an excellent B.Tech team with an electricity-based project ⚡👏. Congratulations to them! We graciously accepted second place and called it a learning experience. Naturally. 😭😂

We built a website, experimented with servos and motors, and explored engineering ideas. Yet the real star was our 🚀 water rocket. Months of work, and apparently, launching something into the air wins every time. 😂

Jokes aside, seeing our efforts recognised was genuinely rewarding 🥳✨. It reminded us that mathematics can go beyond lecture notes and exam papers.

And the juniors ask, "What happened in 2026?" 🤔 Assignments, deadlines, club events, and a spectacular shortage of time. 😭😂 No sequel this year, unfortunately. Juniors, consider this a warning: start early — your future seniors are watching. 😌📚
