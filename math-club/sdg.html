SCIENCE FAIR · 2025

A Water Rocket & a Podium

SCHOOL OF BASIC AND APPLIED SCIENCES · 2ND PLACE

The School of Basic and Applied Sciences hosts the National Science Fair every year, and our team secured 2nd place in the 2025 edition—proof that mathematicians occasionally step out of their notebooks and into the real world.

We arrived presenting our Tamil Nadu State Championship Math Project 2025 with plenty of confidence, only to be edged out by an excellent B.Tech team demonstrating an electricity-based system. We graciously accepted second place, congratulated the winners, and immediately filed the whole experience under character development.

In the weeks leading up to the fair, we built a dedicated project website, wired up servos and motors, and tested several engineering prototypes. Yet when the judges walked the floor, the undisputed crowd-pleaser was our pressurized water rocket. Months of calculations and code, and as it turns out, launching something physically into the air wins every time.

Jokes aside, seeing our department's work recognised on the podium was genuinely rewarding. It was a clear reminder that applied mathematics can travel far beyond lecture notes and exam papers.

A NOTE FOR THE JUNIORS "What happened in 2026?" Assignments, tight deadlines, club events, and a spectacular shortage of hours in the day meant there was no sequel this year. Consider this an official warning: start building early—your seniors are watching.
