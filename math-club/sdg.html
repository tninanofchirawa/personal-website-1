The School of Basic and Applied Sciences hosts the National Science Fair every year, and our team was thrilled to secure 2nd place in the 2025 edition 🏆🔥.

We presented our Tamil Nadu State Championship Math Project 2025 📚✨ and entered the competition with plenty of confidence 😭😂. While we were ultimately outperformed by an outstanding team of B.Tech students with an innovative electricity-based project ⚡👏, we have nothing but admiration for their work.

Beyond the competition, our team built a website, experimented with servos and motors, and explored several hands-on engineering ideas. However, the real crowd favorite was undoubtedly the 🚀 water rocket, which turned out to be an absolute success!

Most importantly, seeing months of effort come together and receiving recognition on such a stage was an incredibly rewarding experience 🥳✨.

To the curious juniors: "What happened in 2026?" 🤔 The answer is simple — assignments, deadlines, events, and a severe shortage of time 😭😂.
