THE ST. JOSEPH'S PREP · 2025

From "You Don't Belong There" to Running the Camp

HOW A SIMPLE PRAYER LED TO SIX DAYS OF CHALK DUST & HUMBLING REALITY

Back in January during my second semester, I stumbled across a strange realization: our department had a Math Club, and none of us from M.Sc. Mathematics were actually in it. Confused, I walked up to a senior faculty member and asked why mathematics students were left out of their own club. The response was blunt: "You don't belong there." It stung. I walked away, said a small, simple prayer about it, and let it go.

Fast forward to May 2025—that very same year. Out of nowhere, the HOD called me in, and just like that, I was appointed President of the Math Club. Talk about an answered prayer. Thanks to the momentum from our science competitions and the backing of our seniors and faculty (yes, even the ones who once told me to mind my own business), we suddenly had the keys to rebuild the club from scratch.

When the new batch arrived, I knew we had a tradition to keep: representing our department at the annual St. Joseph's Math Competition. There was just one small problem—most students perform a complete factory reset on their brains the second their +2 board exams end. When I announced I was going to train them from the ground up, my seniors and friends just laughed. They knew the mountain of work I was volunteering myself for.

Out of roughly 150 members on paper, about 50 showed up to the club, and I pitched daily prep sessions. It started strong with 15 dedicated students and eventually narrowed down to a core squad of 7. Trying to cram massive portions into daily sessions while juggling everyone's upcoming internal exams was rough—and it taught me one thing for certain: I definitely do not want to be a professor.

When competition day arrived at St. Joseph's, other colleges rolled in with entire M.Sc. squads, while we were a brand-new crew treating this as a passionate side hustle. We lost—and yeah, it hurt. Yet watching those seven students study through their internals, put up with all my flaws as a rookie teacher, and show up every day made it one of my favourite memories.

📸 Undoing the post-+2 memory wipe, one daily session at a time
LESSON LEARNED Covering huge portions in a short window is brutal, and teaching is no joke. We didn't bring home the trophy from St. Joseph's, but to the seven who stuck around till the end—you made every chaotic class worth it.
