1. Introduction

Welcome to my personal website. This website showcases my academic interests, projects, learning activities, and experiments in Mathematics, Data Science, Python, and related fields.

I respect the privacy of visitors and participants who interact with my website or submit responses through linked forms. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and what steps can be taken to request its deletion or de-identification.

The goal is to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and to avoid exposing anyone’s personal information.

2. Information Collected Through the Website

Browsing this website does not automatically mean that you are asked to provide personal information directly to me.

Depending on the feature or project you choose to use, information may include:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and phone number, if requested by a form.
  • Survey responses: Answers submitted through Google Forms for educational, research, or data-analysis projects.
  • Location: Where relevant, location information is handled or reported at the city level only. I do not intentionally request or publish exact home addresses, live locations, GPS coordinates, or precise residential locations through these projects.
  • Messages: Information you voluntarily provide when contacting me about a project or website.

A particular form may request only a subset of these details. Please review its questions and purpose before submitting a response.

3. How Personal Information Is Used

Names, email addresses, and phone numbers collected through project forms are treated as confidential information.

They may be used only when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Managing a project or its responses.
  • Contacting a participant about a relevant project.
  • Clarifying a submitted response.
  • Providing requested project-related updates.

I will not intentionally publish these identifying details on this website or in project reports. I will not sell or rent them, or share them with third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

I aim to limit access to identifying information to what is reasonably necessary to manage the project.

4. Surveys, Google Forms, and Research Data

Some projects may use Google Forms to collect survey responses and Google Sheets or similar tools to organize and analyze them.

Participation in surveys is voluntary. Participants may skip questions they are uncomfortable answering and may choose not to submit a form.

The information collected is intended for educational, academic, research, and analytical purposes. It is not intended to create personal profiles, expose individuals, or evaluate participants personally.

The specific information collected depends on the form. Forms may be updated, and additional surveys may be conducted as a project develops. Any significant change to the type of information collected or its intended use should be communicated to participants.

Google and other service providers may process or store information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and technical arrangements. My privacy commitments apply to how I manage and use information within my control; I cannot guarantee the internal practices of external providers.

5. Data Analysis, Anonymity, and Publication

Project results may be presented as charts, graphs, summaries, reports, or other analytical outputs.

When preparing these outputs:

  • Names, email addresses, and phone numbers will not be included in published findings.
  • Location will be presented at the city level, where relevant.
  • Results will be aggregated or appropriately de-identified to focus on general patterns rather than individuals.
  • Information that could reasonably identify a participant through a combination of details will be generalized, grouped, or excluded where necessary.

Removing a name alone does not always guarantee anonymity. Reasonable steps should therefore be taken to remove direct identifiers and reduce the risk of identifying someone from their responses.

Only summarized or appropriately de-identified findings are intended for public presentation. Individual responses will not intentionally be published in a form that identifies the person who submitted them.

6. Use of AI Tools and External Analysis Services

Where AI-assisted tools or other external services are used to help analyze project data, direct identifiers—including names, email addresses, and phone numbers—will be removed or replaced with non-identifying labels such as Participant 1, Participant A, or P1 before information is submitted to those tools, wherever applicable.

Only the information reasonably necessary for the analytical task should be processed. Personal contact details are not intended to be provided to AI tools for analysis.

Replacing identifiers reduces the risk of identification but does not automatically guarantee anonymity. Other details that could reveal a participant's identity should also be considered before data is shared with an external tool.

7. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable steps will be taken to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or misuse.

These steps may include restricting access to response sheets, limiting the use of identifying information, and separating contact details from analytical data where practical.

However, no website, online storage system, or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. I cannot promise absolute protection against every technical failure, security incident, or action by an external service provider.

8. Deletion Requests and De-identification

You may request the deletion of your identifying information by contacting .

When a valid request is received, I will take reasonable steps to remove your identifying information from the project records under my control. Where non-identifying survey data remains useful for completed or ongoing analysis, it may be retained after it has been appropriately de-identified.

This process may include:

  • Removing names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Removing or generalizing location details beyond the city-level information needed for analysis.
  • Removing participant IDs or lookup keys that link a response to an individual.
  • Editing or removing combinations of responses that could reasonably identify a person.
  • Retaining only data that can no longer reasonably be linked to the participant.

The aim is to make the retained data no longer reasonably identifiable as belonging to the person who made the request. This is de-identification and is not necessarily the same as erasing every copy of the original information from every system.

Where information exists in provider-managed systems, backups, or records outside my control, complete or immediate deletion may not be possible. If a limitation prevents full deletion, I will be transparent about that limitation where reasonably possible.

If data has already been irreversibly de-identified and can no longer reasonably be linked to you, it may not be possible to locate and delete your specific response.

9. Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

You may choose not to participate in a survey, skip questions where the form permits, or withdraw from a project by contacting me.

You do not need to provide a reason for withdrawing or requesting de-identification.

If you want a particular response removed, contacting me before the data has been irreversibly de-identified may make it easier to locate the relevant record.

10. Communication and Project Updates

If you provide an email address or phone number, it will be used only for relevant project communication, such as clarification or requested updates.

You may ask to stop receiving project-related messages at any time by contacting me. Your contact details will not intentionally be used for unrelated promotional messages.

11. Cookies, Advertising, and Technical Information

This website does not use Google AdSense to monetize the collected survey information.

Website hosting, embedded content, or third-party services may use technical data or cookies according to their own configurations and policies. The information collected by those services may differ from the information I directly collect through project forms.

I do not claim that every third-party service used by or linked from this website collects no technical information. Please review the relevant provider's privacy policy when using an external service.

12. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites, educational resources, GitHub repositories, Google Forms, or other services.

Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of the external service apply. I am not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of websites operated by other parties.

13. Children's Privacy

Any participant should read the relevant form carefully and provide information only when they are comfortable doing so. Where a project involves minors, appropriate consent and safeguards should be considered before collecting their information.

No participant should submit passwords, banking information, private credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information through project forms.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, projects, forms, or data-handling practices change.

If a significant change affects the information collected, how it is used, or how it is shared, reasonable steps should be taken to communicate the change to affected participants.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.
