1. Introduction

Welcome to my personal website. This website showcases my academic interests, projects, learning activities, and experiments in Mathematics, Data Science, Python, and related fields.

I respect the privacy of visitors and participants who interact with my website or submit responses through linked forms. This policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is protected, and what steps can be taken to request its deletion or de-identification.

The goal is to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and to avoid exposing anyone’s personal information.

2. Information Collected Through the Website

Browsing this website does not automatically mean that you are asked to provide personal information directly to me.

Depending on the feature or project you choose to use, information may include:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and phone number, if requested by a form.
  • Survey responses: Answers submitted through Google Forms for educational, research, or data-analysis projects.
  • Location: Where relevant, location information is handled or reported at the city level only. I do not intentionally request or publish exact home addresses, live locations, GPS coordinates, or precise residential locations through these projects.
  • Messages: Information you voluntarily provide when contacting me about a project or website.

A particular form may request only a subset of these details. Please review its questions and purpose before submitting a response.

3. How Personal Information Is Used

Names, email addresses, and phone numbers collected through project forms are treated as confidential information.

They may be used only when reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

  • Managing a project or its responses.
  • Contacting a participant about a relevant project.
  • Clarifying a submitted response.
  • Providing requested project-related updates.

I will not intentionally publish these identifying details on this website or in project reports. I will not sell or rent them, or share them with third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated purposes.

I aim to limit access to identifying information to what is reasonably necessary to manage the project.

4. Surveys, Google Forms, and Research Data

Some projects may use Google Forms to collect survey responses and Google Sheets or similar tools to organize and analyze them.

Participation in surveys is voluntary. Participants may skip questions they are uncomfortable answering and may choose not to submit a form.

The information collected is intended for educational, academic, research, and analytical purposes. It is not intended to create personal profiles, expose individuals, or evaluate participants personally.

The specific information collected depends on the form. Forms may be updated, and additional surveys may be conducted as a project develops. Any significant change to the type of information collected or its intended use should be communicated to participants.

Google and other service providers may process or store information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and technical arrangements. My privacy commitments apply to how I manage and use information within my control; I cannot guarantee the internal practices of external providers.

5. Data Analysis & Anonymity

Project findings may be presented through charts, graphs, reports, summaries, and other analytical outputs. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, and other direct identifiers will not be included in published findings. Results will be presented in aggregated or appropriately de-identified formats, focusing on general trends and patterns rather than individual participants.

Any details that could potentially identify a participant, either directly or through a combination of responses, will be generalized or excluded where necessary. Only summarized or appropriately de-identified findings are intended for public presentation. Individual responses will not intentionally be published in a manner that reveals the identity of the participant.

6. Use of AI Tools and External Analysis Services

When AI tools or external services are used for project analysis, direct identifiers such as names, email addresses, and phone numbers will be removed or replaced with non-identifying labels such as Participant 1 or P1 before sharing the data, wherever applicable.

Only information necessary for the intended analytical task will be shared. Personal contact details and unnecessary sensitive information will not intentionally be submitted to AI tools or external services for analysis.

Removing direct identifiers does not guarantee complete anonymity. Other details that could potentially reveal a participant's identity will also be reviewed and minimized before information is shared with external tools.

7. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures will be taken to protect collected information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. Access to response sheets will be restricted, identifying information will be handled carefully, and contact details will be kept separate from analytical data wherever practical.

While appropriate precautions will be taken to safeguard information, no website, online storage platform, or electronic transmission method can guarantee complete security. Therefore, absolute protection against all technical failures, security breaches, or incidents involving external service providers cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Deletion Requests and De-identification

Participants may request the deletion of their personal or identifying information by contacting . Requests should include sufficient information to identify the relevant submission without unnecessarily disclosing additional personal details.

Upon receiving a verifiable request, reasonable steps will be taken to locate and remove the relevant identifying information from project records under my control. Where the underlying response data remains necessary for legitimate analytical purposes, it may be retained only after appropriate de-identification, subject to applicable requirements.

De-identification may involve:

  • Removing direct identifiers, including names, email addresses, and phone numbers.
  • Removing or generalizing location and other potentially identifying details.
  • Deleting participant IDs, lookup keys, or other references that connect responses to individuals.
  • Reviewing combinations of responses that could indirectly reveal a participant's identity.
  • Retaining only information that cannot reasonably be associated with an identifiable individual.

The objective is to remove the participant's identifiable information while preserving only data that can no longer reasonably be linked to them. De-identification is distinct from deletion: it does not necessarily erase every copy of the original information or its associated records.

Deletion requests apply to information within my reasonable control. Data held in third-party platforms, provider-managed backups, system logs, or other external records may be subject to separate retention and deletion procedures. Consequently, immediate or complete removal from every external system cannot be guaranteed.

Once data has been permanently de-identified to the extent that it can no longer reasonably be linked to a participant, identifying and deleting that individual's specific response may no longer be technically possible.

9. Voluntary Participation and Withdrawal

You may choose not to participate in a survey, skip questions where the form permits, or withdraw from a project by contacting me.

You do not need to provide a reason for withdrawing or requesting de-identification.

If you want a particular response removed, contacting me before the data has been irreversibly de-identified may make it easier to locate the relevant record.

10. Communication and Project Updates

If you provide an email address or phone number, it will be used only for relevant project communication, such as clarification or requested updates.

You may ask to stop receiving project-related messages at any time by contacting me. Your contact details will not intentionally be used for unrelated promotional messages.

11. Cookies, Advertising, and Technical Information

This website does not use Google AdSense to monetize the collected survey information.

Website hosting, embedded content, or third-party services may use technical data or cookies according to their own configurations and policies. The information collected by those services may differ from the information I directly collect through project forms.

I do not claim that every third-party service used by or linked from this website collects no technical information. Please review the relevant provider's privacy policy when using an external service.

12. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites, educational resources, GitHub repositories, Google Forms, or other services.

Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of the external service apply. I am not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of websites operated by other parties.

13. Children's Privacy

Our forms and services are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request, collect, or process personal information from children under 13. Children in this age group must not submit personal information through our forms.

If we become aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been collected, we will take appropriate steps to delete it. If you believe this has occurred, please contact us immediately.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically to reflect changes in the website, projects, data collection methods, processing practices, or applicable requirements.

Where significant changes affect the types of information collected, its intended use, storage, or disclosure, reasonable efforts will be made to notify affected participants and communicate the relevant changes before implementing them, where practicable.

The latest version, together with its effective or revision date, will be published on this page. Continued use of the forms or services after an update does not override any consent or rights required under applicable law.
