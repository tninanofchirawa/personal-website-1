Hi, I am Timothy. I’m currently a 2nd year MSc. Mathematics (Integrated) student at Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science. My main goal right now is to move towards Data Science and eventually land a great job in the field.

The college is honestly great, and the staff are super supportive 🥳

Right now, our department mainly has 3 courses:
  • PhD Program in Mathematics (I don’t know too much about it yet — but if you ask, I can definitely find out 😅)
  • MSc. Mathematics (Integrated) — 4 year course
  • BSc. Mathematics and Data Science — 3 year course

Personally, I would recommend the Pure Math course more. One big reason is that we have a really strong CSE department. Math students usually end up making friends with CSE and Aerospace students, and that naturally pulls you into hackathons, coding projects, ML, research ideas, and a lot of tech-related opportunities. So even if you start with Pure Math, you still get exposed to programming and Data Science through the people and environment around you.
